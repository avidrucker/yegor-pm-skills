## [0.2.0] -- 2026-06-26

### Added
- "The architect's authority" section: the architect's only boss is the
  requirements (argue from the spec, amend it rather than from authority); the
  two instruments (filing bugs + reviewing code) are the *only* enforcement
  tools; "what if the architect is wrong" → eyes in proportion to risk (more
  independent reviewers for higher-stakes calls), never a committee override;
  decides-without-persuading but is fully accountable.
- Cross-references to yegor-review and yegor-personas; this layer supplies the
  personas authority ladder (architect = rung 5, requirements = rung 1).
- Distilled from Yegor Bugayenko's "Who Is an Architect?" (2014), "Two
  Instruments of a Software Architect" (2015), and "What if the Architect is
  Wrong?" (2019).
- Deep reference: research/philosophy_21_architect_authority.md
