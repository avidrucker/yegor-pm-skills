# Key Takeaways

**Video:** SIMBA — Simplified Management By Artifacts, at Heisenbug'2021
**Link:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2IwBc9UI4Sg
**Speaker:** Yegor Bugayenko, Head of R&D Lab, Huawei Moscow Research Center

## Key Takeaways for quick navigation

- **00:00** Opening — Yegor introduces himself and the SIMBA framework for managing distributed R&D teams
- **02:30** The problem: managing professors and researchers who are smart, busy, enjoy their work, and are expert excuse-makers
- **06:00** Why traditional contract-based management failed — they delivered documentation instead of practical results
- **09:00** The four elements of SIMBA: Plan, Monday Reports, Status Calls, Demos
- **11:00** Element 1 — Plan: artifact-based (not activity-based), one-page Google Doc per project, each artifact has owner + reviewer
- **19:00** Element 2 — Monday Report: three sections (last week achievements, next week plan, risks), sent to all stakeholders
- **27:00** Element 3 — Status Calls: 30-min Zoom, review plan, ask owners status, use peer pressure (not punishment)
- **33:00** Element 4 — Demos: voluntary, conference-style presentations, recorded and published, people love to show off
- **40:00** Four problems: (1) hard to decompose research, (2) reporting results vs actions, (3) excuse-making, (4) scared to talk about risks
- **45:00** Risk management as self-protection: the more risks you report, the more protection you get
- **50:00** Escalation technique: don't complain ("not enough staff"), frame as risk ("if this continues, project fails")
- **55:00** Daily reports were tried and removed (caused uproar) — only useful in "crunch" situations
- **58:00** Written vs verbal reports: written is harder to sweet-talk, easier to forward and verify
- **62:00** SIMBA is for R&D, not development — development needs Kanban, research needs flexible artifact-based management
- **65:00** Risk management is a hard skill — many managers are not ready to read and act on risks
